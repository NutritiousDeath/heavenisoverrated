The Signal

Every city has a frequency underneath it — the one nobody's supposed to hear. Pills on the Parmesian is that frequency. Ethereal vocals, purple lightning, a signal that keeps transmitting long after everything else has gone quiet.

The Sound

Where Heaven is Overrated speaks entirely through instruments, Pills on the Parmesian is voice-first — vocals run through layers of processing until they stop sounding human and start sounding like something broadcasting from somewhere else.

The Darker Side of Life

The debut full album, The Darker Side of Life, is streaming now. It's the first transmission — the one that started before any of the crossover projects existed.
